Tapped Support

Tapped shows a red, yellow, or green summary of US drinking-water compliance records for the place you are in. It uses public EPA records, not a fresh lab test.

Need help? Email support@easternlabs.app. Include your ZIP code and a short description, and we will reply as soon as we can.

About the results

Green, yellow, and red reflect whether EPA records show recent or open health-based violations for the water system serving the area. The full rules are in the methodology.

Tapped summarizes public regulatory records. It is not a water test and does not guarantee that water is safe to drink. For concerns about your own tap, contact your local water utility or a medical professional.

Privacy

Tapped collects only the five-digit ZIP code it uses to look up records. See the privacy policy.
